Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Pick & Race is a free set of classroom name-picker tools. This page explains what information is and is not collected when you use the site.

Names you enter

The names and lists you type into the pickers are processed in your browser to run the animation. They are not sent to our servers and are not stored after you close or reload the page. The "no repeats" history is also kept only while the page is open.

What the site stores on your device

The site saves one small setting in your browser's local storage: whether you have muted sound effects. It contains no personal information. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Advertising and cookies

This site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including ads based on your earlier visits to this or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to our site and other sites on the internet.

  • You can manage or opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • You can learn more about how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent message before personalized ads are used, and can change their choice later. Visitors in some US states are given opt-out choices where required by law.

Third-party services

To load the pages we use Google Fonts for typefaces, and one tool (Duck Race, PixiJS edition) loads open-source libraries from the cdnjs content delivery network. The site is hosted on Vercel, which, like most hosts, may keep standard server logs such as IP address and browser type for security and operations. These providers have their own privacy policies.

Children

The site is made to be used by teachers and students. We do not ask for accounts, names, emails or any other personal information, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Teachers and parents who have questions about advertising and children can contact us using the details below.
