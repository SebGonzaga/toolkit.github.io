How teachers use Pick & Race

Pick & Race turns a plain class list into a short, fair and fun moment. Paste your roster, press the button, and an animated duck race, wheel, claw machine or bracket decides who answers, who leads, or who goes first. Every tool shares one engine, so each name has the same chance unless you choose to change the odds.

Why a picker helps

  • It removes the feeling that the teacher always calls on the same students.
  • It makes cold-calling feel like a game instead of a test.
  • It gives you a quick, repeatable way to form groups, order presentations and run tournaments.

Tips

Turn on No repeats so everyone gets a turn before any name returns, use Projector mode for big screens, and try the Weighted Picker when you want to quietly give quieter students a better chance. Read more on the About page.
