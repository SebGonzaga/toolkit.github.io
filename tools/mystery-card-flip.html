About the Mystery Card Flip Name Picker

Every name on your list gets its own card. Press Deal the cards and they appear face-up so everyone can see them, then flip over and shuffle around the table. When the shuffle stops, you (or the teacher) choose a card, and it turns over to reveal the winner. The shuffle length slider controls how long the shuffling lasts.

Ways to use it in class

  • Let a student pick a card to choose who answers next.
  • Choose who plays first in a card game.
  • Run a calm, hands-on reveal for a smaller group.
  • Decide the order for oral reports, one card at a time.

A lesson idea

Make each card a question by listing question topics instead of names, then shuffle and let a student choose a card to see which comes up. Choosing their own card makes a plain list of discussion prompts feel exciting. You can also use it for show-and-tell, letting the class watch the shuffle while they wait to see who shares first.

Good to know

A longer shuffle builds suspense, and a shorter one is better when time is short. Turn on No repeats and everyone gets a turn before any name comes up a second time, which keeps it fair over a whole week. Projector mode switches to bigger, higher-contrast graphics so the back row can see the cards.

Questions teachers ask

Are the cards shuffled fairly?
Yes. Every shuffle is a real random shuffle, and the cards are face-down when you choose, so nobody can tell whose name is on which card.
Can I see all the names face-up?
Yes, at the start. All the cards are shown face-up for a few seconds, then they flip over and get shuffled.
