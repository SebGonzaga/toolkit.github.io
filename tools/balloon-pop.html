Balloon Pop Name Picker

Balloons float and drift around the sky as they inflate — but nobody can tell who's ahead until the very end. Watch for stretching skin, slow leaks, a bird (or dart) circling overhead, and a final-two squeeze with a half-second of silence before the bang. Works for 2 names or 40.

About the Balloon Pop Name Picker

Each name rides on its own balloon. Press Inflate & release and the balloons float, drift and wobble wilder the bigger they get. The first balloon to pop wins, and its name is the pick. The race length slider controls how long the inflating lasts.

Ways to use it in class

  • Pick a birthday helper or a class leader.
  • Choose who gets to hand out materials.
  • Run a quick opener before a spelling or maths game.
  • Decide who goes first in an indoor-recess game.

A lesson idea

Balloons suit short, cheerful moments such as the start of a lesson or the end of the day. Put the names of helpers for the next day's jobs on the balloons, pick one balloon per job, and let the pops decide. The suspense of the wobble makes even routine chores feel like a game.

Why you can't guess the winner

The leader's glow stays hidden until the last stretch, a few other balloons creak and wobble like they're about to go, and one big balloon may spring a slow leak, shrink, and then recover. A bird or dart sweeps past a few balloons, then returns for the real pop. In the final seconds the last two balloons are shoved together under a darkening sky, the sound drops out, and then the bang lands.

Mystery names mode

Turn on Mystery names mode and the balloons carry only numbers. When the winner pops, a card drops out of it, flutters down in a shower of confetti, and flips over to reveal the name.

Good to know

Balloons are simply the costume for the same fair draw used across the site. Turn on No repeats and everyone gets a turn before any name comes up a second time, which keeps it fair over a whole week. Projector mode switches to bigger, higher-contrast graphics so the back row can see the result.

Questions teachers ask

Do the balloons really pop?
They pop as part of the animation; nothing is lost and you can run it again instantly.
Can I change the names between rounds?
Yes. Edit the list in the box and run it again.
