About the Classroom Noise Detector

The Classroom Noise Detector listens through your device's microphone and turns the volume of the room into a simple live meter. Green means calm, yellow means the noise is creeping up, and red means it is over the limit you set. A strike is added when the room stays too loud for longer than your grace period.

Ways to use it in class

  • Keep a silent test or independent reading time on track without having to shush anyone.
  • Give group work a target volume and let the class watch the meter themselves.
  • Run a quiet-class challenge: try to finish the lesson with fewer than three strikes.
  • Use projector mode so the whole room can see the traffic light.

Setting it up

Pick a preset for the activity, then nudge the noise limit until the meter feels right for your room. If the meter hardly moves, raise the microphone sensitivity. Every room and device is different, so it is a good idea to test it with a normal speaking voice first.

Good to know

This is a relative classroom guide, not a calibrated sound-level meter, so the numbers are not decibels and should not be used for health or safety measurements. Your browser must allow microphone access, and the page needs to be opened over https (or from your own computer) for that to work.

Questions teachers ask

Does it record my class?
No. It only measures loudness in your browser. No audio is saved or uploaded.
The meter is too jumpy.
Increase the grace period and lower the sensitivity a little.
Can I use it on a projector?
Yes. Use Projector mode for a big, full-screen light and face.
