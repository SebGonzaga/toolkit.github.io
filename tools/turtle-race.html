    About the Turtle Race Name Picker

    Each name becomes a turtle on a rolling hill trail. Turtles are famously slow, so this race is a long, funny build-up to a winner. Add your list, press Begin the (slow) race, and watch the camera follow the leader up the mountain while the progress bar at the top tracks every turtle along the climb, until one shuffles over the summit line.

    What the turtles get up to

    • Shell slides: when a turtle surges, it tucks into its shell and slides forward with speed lines behind it.
    • Naps: when a turtle tires, it starts to doze off, and a hare snoozes beside the trail. Once the leader passes, the hare wakes up and chases, a little too late.
    • Hills and puddles: turtles tilt to the slope and splash through puddles on the way up.

    Ways to use it in class

    • Choose a student for a task that benefits from a bit of suspense.
    • Give a restless class something calm and silly to watch for a minute.
    • Pick the order for a quiet reading rotation.
    • Decide which group gets to go first in a team activity.

    A lesson idea

    Slow races are great for transitions. Start the turtles while students pack away or line up, and announce that the winner leads the line when the race ends. The long, silly build-up gives everyone a minute to settle, and the winner has a clear job to do straight away. The hill also makes a nice hook for a quick chat about slopes, speed and the tortoise and the hare fable.

    Good to know

    Use the race length slider to keep the wait short when time is tight. Turn on No repeats and everyone gets a turn before any name comes up a second time, which keeps it fair over a whole week. Projector mode switches to bigger, higher-contrast graphics and labels so the back row can see the result. With a big class, only the leading turtles are labelled so the screen stays readable.

    Questions teachers ask

    Is the slowest-looking turtle ever the winner?
    Any turtle can win. The outcome is chosen fairly before the race and the animation simply plays it out.
    Does a shell slide or a nap change who wins?
    No. Shell slides and naps are just how the race is drawn. They show the pace the turtle was already going to run, so they are fun to watch but never tilt the odds.
    Can I stop a race early?
    Reload the page to start again; your names stay in the box until you change them.
